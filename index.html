Ch. 05 — Questions

Frequently asked,
plainly answered.

The questions teams ask us most often, before they sign up. If yours isn't here, write to us — a real person will reply.

What is HR Assist?

HR Assist is a complete cloud HRMS (Human Resources Management System) that covers the entire employee lifecycle — payroll, attendance, leave, recruitment, expenses, timesheets, exit and more — in one integrated platform with 11 modules.

How much does HR Assist cost?

The Grove plan is ₹100 per employee per month and includes all 11 modules with no add-ons. The Canopy plan offers custom pricing for enterprise needs such as SSO, multi-entity consolidation, and private cloud deployment.

Does HR Assist support Indian payroll compliance?

Yes. HR Assist handles Indian statutory deductions and filings out of the box, including PF, ESI, Professional Tax (PT), and TDS, along with auto-generated payslips and Form 16.

Is HR Assist suitable for small businesses?

Yes. HR Assist is built for teams from 1–10 employees up to enterprise. The Grove plan at ₹100/employee/month gives small teams full access to all 11 modules with no feature gating.

How is data secured in HR Assist?

HR Assist is multi-tenant with row-level security so each customer's data is isolated. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and the platform offers role-level audit logs and versioned history. See our security page for the full architecture.

Can I migrate from another HR system?

Yes. The Canopy plan includes a dedicated onboarding specialist and migration team. Even on Grove, every module ships with CSV import/export so moving from spreadsheets, Zoho People, Keka, GreytHR, or DarwinBox is straightforward.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 30 days of full Grove access with no credit card required. You can walk away at any time and export your data.
